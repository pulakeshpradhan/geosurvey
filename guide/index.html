Set up GeoSurvey for your team

Two things make the app work: a free AI key (so photos and recordings fill the form) and a Google Sheet (where every answer, photo and recording is saved). This guide shows every click.

You need: a Google account, a computer for steps 1–2, and the phones that will collect data. Use → / ← or swipe to move between slides.

Step 1 of 4 2 min

Get the free AI key

  1. 1Open aistudio.google.com/app/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. 2Click Create API key.
  3. 3Click Copy. The key starts with AIza. Paste it somewhere safe (a note) — you need it in Step 3.
Treat the key like a password. Do not post it publicly. You can create a new one any time.

Step 2 of 4 · part B 1 min

Paste the code and set your password

  1. 1Open github.com/pulakeshpradhan/geosurvey → folder backend → file Code.gs. Click the copy icon at the top right of the file.
  2. 2Paste into the empty Apps Script page.
  3. 3Near the top find the line with ADMIN_TOKEN. Replace change-me-to-a-long-secret with a password of your choice. Keep the quotes.
  4. 4Press Ctrl+S to save.
This password later opens the “Collected data” view and lets you publish questionnaires to all phones.

Step 2 of 4 · part C 1 min

Allow Google Drive (so photos and recordings can be saved)

  1. 1In the Apps Script toolbar there is a dropdown that says myFunction or doPost. Change it to authorizeDrive.
  2. 2Click ▶ Run.
  3. 3A window says “Authorization required”: click Review permissions → choose your account → Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow. (Google shows “unsafe” for every personal script — it is your own code.)
  4. 4The log at the bottom shows “Drive authorised” and a link to the new folder GeoSurvey Photos in your Drive.
Do not skip this. Without it the answers still arrive, but photos and recordings are not stored.

Step 2 of 4 · part D 1 min

Publish the script and copy its address

  1. 1Click Deploy (top right) → New deployment.
  2. 2Click the gear icon next to “Select type” and choose Web app.
  3. 3Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy.
  4. 4Click Copy under Web app URL. It ends with /exec. Keep it with the AI key.
Later, whenever you paste a newer Code.gs: Deploy → Manage deployments → ✎ → Version: New version → Deploy. Otherwise the old code keeps running.

Step 3 of 4 1 min per phone

Put the key and the address into the app

  1. 1Open pulakeshpradhan.github.io/geosurvey on the phone (Chrome or Safari). Tap the ⚙️ icon at the top right.
  2. 2Paste the AI key into Gemini API key → tap Test connection → it must say OK.
  3. 3Paste the Web app URL into Database endpoint → tap Test database → it must say Backend OK … Drive folder ready.
  4. 4Type the Enumerator name, choose the Interview language, tap Save.
Every enumerator does this on their own phone with the same key and same URL. All phones then write into the same Sheet.

Step 4 of 4 1 min

Choose the questionnaire

The first time, the app asks how to start. Pick one:

  1. AUse sample questionnaire — a complete household survey. Fastest way to begin.
  2. BDesign your own — add sections and questions like Google Forms.
  3. CUpload paper questionnaire — PDF or photos of your printed form; it is converted exactly as printed. Then Improve with AI if you want.

Change it any time with the Edit button. Apply = this phone. Publish to team = every phone gets it (asks for your password from Step 2).

In the field

One household in five steps

  1. 1Location fills itself. Wait until it shows e.g. ±8 m. Nothing to press.
  2. 2Capture photo the house, kitchen, water source, ID or paper form — and/or Record the interview in any language.
  3. 3Analyze & fill — the AI fills the answers in English. Answers in yellow are AI-filled: check and correct.
  4. 4Each section has its own 📷 ✨ 🎙 buttons. Recording with a section mic fills that section as soon as you stop.
  5. 5Submit. Saved on the phone, sent to the Sheet; no internet → sent later by itself. PDF prints an A4 copy.

Where everything is saved

Your data stays in your Google account

PhoneRecords tab keeps a copy of answers, photos, recordings until you remove them
→
Google Sheetone row per household, tab “Responses”
+
Drive folder “GeoSurvey Photos”photos, recordings (.webm), transcripts (.txt); links in the Sheet

In the app: Records → column Files in Drive shows a green “in Drive” with links, or a red “not in Drive” with an Upload files button that re-sends them. Settings → Test database always shows a direct link to the folder.

Downloads: Records → CSV, JSON, SPSS (.sav), KMZ (Google Earth). Analysis tab → automatic statistics and a plain-language report. Edit → Collected data (your password) → all households from all phones.

If something does not work

Quick fixes

You seeDo this
“AI error” on Analyze & fillSettings → Test connection. If it fails, create a new key in AI Studio and paste it again.
Test database: “old Code.gs”Paste the latest Code.gs, then Deploy → Manage deployments → ✎ → New version → Deploy.
“permission to call DriveApp”Step 2C was skipped: run authorizeDrive, click Allow, deploy a new version, then Records → Upload files.
“Locating…” never finishesAllow location when the phone asks; move near a window or outside; tap ⟳ Refresh.
Camera or microphone does not openAllow camera and microphone for the site in the phone’s browser settings.
App looks old after an updateClose it completely and open again.
